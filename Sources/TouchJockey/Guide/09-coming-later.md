# Coming later

These techniques come next as Touch Jockey grows. Each one will start simple and level up in the same way.

- **Beatmatching by ear**: turning SYNC off and matching tempos with the pitch control while listening in headphones. It's the classic DJ skill, and SYNC will stay available as the starter.
- **Cue points and loops**: jumping to a track's drop, and looping an intro to stretch a transition.
- **Headphone cueing**: hearing the next track before the crowd does.
- **Key mixing**: choosing tracks whose keys sound good together, using the Camelot wheel.
- **Effects**: filters, echoes and builds.

Until then, keep practising handoffs and mixer transitions. They're the moves you'll use in every set.
