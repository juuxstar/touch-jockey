# Level up: the crossfader

## The technique

The **crossfader** blends the two decks with one control: left is deck A, right is deck B. In the centre, both play at full volume. House and techno DJs mostly leave it in the middle and mix with the channel faders and EQ. Scratch and hip-hop DJs use it for fast cuts.

It's useful for:

- **Quick cuts**: snap from one track to the other on a downbeat, for an abrupt, energetic change.
- **Safety**: parking it on one side means the other deck can't be heard by accident while you prepare it.

## In Touch Jockey

The crossfader sits along the bottom of the mixer, on the Mac and on the phone. Double-click (or double-tap on the phone) to centre it.

## Practice

With both tracks playing in sync, cut from A to B on the ONE of a phrase. Then try cutting back and forth every bar for 4 bars before settling on B. It's a fun trick to use sparingly.
