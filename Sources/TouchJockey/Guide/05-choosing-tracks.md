# Choosing the next track

A smooth transition starts before you touch a control: with the right next track.

## The technique

- **Tempo**: tracks within a few BPM of each other blend naturally. SYNC can bridge bigger gaps, but a track sped up by 10% can sound wrong.
- **Energy**: decide whether the next track keeps the energy steady, lifts it, or gives the dance floor a breather. Plan a few tracks ahead.
- **Key**: some tracks clash harmonically. Key mixing comes in a later chapter; for now, trust your ears.

## In Touch Jockey

- The **dot** next to each track in the library compares its tempo with the track playing now: **green** within 3%, **gold** within 6%, **red** further off.
- A **?** after a BPM means the beat was hard to detect. Sync may drift with that track, so pick something else while you're learning.
- **Recent** lists the tracks you loaded lately, which is handy for building a set from your favourites.

## Practice

Pick five tracks that go well together, all green against each other. Play them as a short set using handoffs, and decide the order by energy: start calm and finish strong.
