# Set up and look around

## Load two tracks

1. In the **Library** on the left, double-click a track. It goes into **deck A**, the outgoing deck: the one the crowd hears.
2. Double-click a second track with a similar BPM (look for a **green** dot). It goes into **deck B**, the incoming deck: the one you'll bring in next.

Deck A always stays on the left and deck B on the right. Only their roles swap, from outgoing to incoming and back.

## Connect your phone

Your phone is the instrument: you'll blend with your fingers.

1. Open **Settings** (⌘,), or click **connected** in the status line, and choose **Connect a phone**.
2. Scan the QR code with your iPhone camera. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac.
3. In Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open Touch Jockey from the new icon for full screen.

## The screen

- **Decks A and B**: each has **CUE**, **PLAY/PAUSE** and **SYNC** (or **MASTER** for the deck that sets the tempo).
- **Timeline**: both tracks lined up beat for beat. The white line is now.
- **Mixer**: on the Mac, it shows what the phone is doing. Later, it becomes the real mixer.
- **ⓘ icons**: click one whenever you're unsure what something is.

## Practice

Press **PLAY** on deck A and just listen for a minute. Watch the waveform scroll past the white line, and notice the bar counter on the deck card.
