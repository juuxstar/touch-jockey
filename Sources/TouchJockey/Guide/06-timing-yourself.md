# Level up: time the bass swap yourself

Until now, a flick waited for the next bar. Real mixers don't wait: the swap happens the instant you move.

## The technique

Hitting the downbeat yourself is a basic DJ skill. Count the bar in your head and move **on the ONE**, slightly early rather than late, because the movement takes a moment.

## In Touch Jockey

Turn it on in **Settings → Your skills → Bass-swap timing → Free**. A flick now lands the moment you make it.

1. Count the bars along with the music.
2. Flick LOWS on the ONE of a new phrase.
3. Listen: did the bass drop together with the kick, or did it slip?

## Practice

Do ten handoffs with Free timing. If more than two sound off, go back to Snap for a while. There's no shame in it; the point is to learn.
