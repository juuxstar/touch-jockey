# Your first transition: the handoff

This is the core move of house and techno DJing: hand the music over from one track to the next, **part by part**, over a phrase or two.

## The technique

Music has three main bands:

- **Lows**: kick drum and bass.
- **Mids**: chords, vocals, synths.
- **Highs**: hi-hats, cymbals, shimmer.

DJs blend band by band. The new track's highs and mids come in gradually, then the basslines **swap**: the old track's bass out and the new track's bass in, at the same moment, on a downbeat. Two basslines playing together sound muddy, which is why the bass swap is the moment that matters.

## In Touch Jockey

The phone shows three **handoff lanes**: HIGHS, MIDS and LOWS. Each lane moves one band from deck A to deck B.

1. With deck A playing, tap **Bring in B next phrase**. B starts at the next phrase, in sync and silent.
2. Over the next 8–16 bars, slowly slide **HIGHS**, then **MIDS**, towards B. You'll hear B's hats and melody arrive.
3. On a phrase line, **flick LOWS** towards B. The bass swaps exactly on the next bar.
4. Finish the other lanes. When every lane is on B, B becomes the playing deck and A stops.

> Hold the phone upright and you can slide all three lanes with three fingers.

## Practice

Do ten handoffs, A to B and back to B to A. Try them at different speeds: one over 8 bars, one over 32. Listen for the moment that sounds best for the bass swap.

## Level up

When you can time the bass swap to a phrase without thinking, go to **Settings → Your skills** and set **Bass-swap timing** to **Free**. From then on, a flick lands the moment you make it, so the timing is yours (chapter 6).
