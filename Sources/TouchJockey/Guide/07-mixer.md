# Level up: the mixer

The handoff lanes always trade one deck against the other. A real mixer controls each deck separately, which opens up new moves.

## The technique

Each deck has its own **channel strip**:

- **EQ**: HIGH, MID and LOW for that deck alone. The centre is normal (0 dB); down cuts the band, all the way to a **kill** (silent).
- **Volume fader**: how loud the deck is.

With separate controls you can do things lanes can't:

- **Cut early, bring in late**: drop A's bass a bar before B's comes in. That gap builds tension before the drop.
- **Both full**: let both tracks' highs play together for a richer blend.
- **Fader rides**: bring a whole track in slowly with its volume fader over 16 or 32 bars, which is typical for melodic techno.

## In Touch Jockey

Set **Blend control** to **Mixer** in **Settings → Your skills**. The phone switches to a mixer: deck A's strip on the left, deck B's on the right, and a crossfader along the bottom.

**Go practice** and **Reset** set the mixer up for the first step: every EQ in the centre, the outgoing deck's fader up and the incoming deck's fader down.

A typical mixer transition:

1. Before starting B, turn **B's LOW** all the way down (kill).
2. Start B on a phrase. Raise **B's volume** over 8 bars, with its lows still off.
3. On a phrase line, **kill A's LOW** and raise **B's LOW** to the centre at the same moment. That's the bass swap, done with two fingers.
4. Ease A's MID and HIGH down, then **pause A**. B becomes the master.

## Practice

Repeat the four steps until they flow. Then try the tension trick: kill A's bass one bar *before* bringing B's in.
