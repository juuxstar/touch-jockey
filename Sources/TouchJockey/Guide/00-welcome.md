# Welcome

Touch Jockey teaches DJing one technique at a time.

Real DJ gear asks you to do many things at once: keep two tracks in time, count bars, ride faders, turn EQ knobs, all with both hands. Touch Jockey starts you on **simpler controls** that handle the fiddly parts, so you can learn what matters first: **when** to mix and **how** a mix should sound.

Each chapter follows the same shape:

- **The technique**: what DJs do and why it works.
- **In Touch Jockey**: how to do it with the starter control.
- **Practice**: a short exercise to try right away.
- **Level up**: once it feels natural, switch to the real control in **Settings → Your skills**.

> Nothing is cheating here. A crossfader is a shortcut for two volume faders, and SYNC replaced beatmatching by ear years ago. The skill is in your choices and your timing, and those stay yours from the first chapter.

Work through the chapters in order, and mark each one done when you've practised it. You can come back to any chapter at any time from **Help → Touch Jockey Guide**.
