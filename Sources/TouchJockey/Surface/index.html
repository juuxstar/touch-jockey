How the mixer works

Deck A's channel is on the left and deck B's on the right, with the volume faders in the middle.

HIGH, MID and LOW turn one part of a track up or down. At the bottom it's killed (silent); the centre line is unchanged.

Classic blend: bring the new track in with its LOW killed, then swap the basslines on a phrase.

The crossfader along the bottom blends A and B. In the centre both play at full.

Drag to move, double-tap to reset. To finish, stop the old track on the laptop.

With two phones, give each one a deck with the A / B buttons under the crossfader, so each channel gets the whole screen.

How the lanes work

Each lane moves one part of the music between deck A and deck B: A at the top and B at the bottom when the phone is upright, A left and B right when it's sideways. Decks never move: after A hands over to B, the next blend goes back from B to A.

Upright, the three lanes sit side by side, so three fingers can each slide one.

Drag a lane to hand that band over. Move highs and mids slowly over a phrase.

Flick a lane and it swaps exactly on the next bar. Use it on LOWS for a clean bass swap.

Two-finger tap anywhere sends everything back to the track that is playing.
