How the lanes work

Each lane moves one part of the music between deck A and deck B: A at the top and B at the bottom when the phone is upright, A left and B right when it's sideways. Decks never move: after A hands over to B, the next blend goes back from B to A.

Upright, the three lanes sit side by side, so three fingers can each slide one.

Drag a lane to hand that band over. Move highs and mids slowly over a phrase.

Flick a lane and it swaps exactly on the next bar. Use it on LOWS for a clean bass swap.

Two-finger tap anywhere sends everything back to the track that is playing.
